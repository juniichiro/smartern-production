# Supervisor & Head Supervisor Guide

Supervisors govern daily workflows, assign tasks, and monitor attendance metrics for interns assigned to their respective offices.

---

## Tasks & Accomplishments

Supervisors manage the internship lifecycle through the task module:
- **Task Assignment**: Assign tasks to individual interns or groups.
- **Verification**: Review submitted accomplishment reports and mark tasks as Completed, Pending Revision, or Overdue.
- **Feedback**: Leave notes on submissions to guide interns through revisions.

---

## Announcements & Communication

Supervisors can broadcast announcements to all deployed interns in their department:
- **Distribution Channels**: Notifications are distributed in real-time via the in-app message system and dispatched as emails.
- **Targeting**: Automatically targets only the interns active within the supervisor's specific office.

---

## Office Analytics

The Supervisor Dashboard offers localized charts:
- **Intern Counts**: Current headcount active in the office.
- **Task Analytics**: Real-time completion rates and output metrics.
- **Attendance Patterns**: Logs tracking late clock-ins, early clock-outs, and absences.

---

## Head Supervisor (Ms. Mal-in) Privileges

The **Head Supervisor** has higher authorization level to manage placement across the entire SDO QC division:

### 1. Rete Placement Console
- Access the Placement tool to run the matching engine on incoming student profiles.
- Inspect percentage matching scores and adjust office rules.
- Export placement tables to spreadsheets or PDF.

### 2. Multi-Office Analytics
- View consolidated charts showing intern distribution, performance, and compliance metrics across all registered offices.
