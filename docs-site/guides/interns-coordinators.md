# Intern & Coordinator Guide

Learn how interns document their internship hours and how school coordinators monitor their students' progress.

---

## Intern Workspace

### 1. Account Onboarding
- **First-Time Password Reset**: Upon logging in with your temporary generated password, you will be prompted to set a new password meeting the complexity requirements (min 12 characters, mixing numbers, symbols, uppercase and lowercase letters).
- **MFA Setup**: You must scan the presented QR code with your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) and verify the 6-digit TOTP code before accessing your workspace.

### 2. Daily Attendance (GPS Clock-In/Out)
- Navigate to the **Attendance** tab.
- Allow the browser to access your device's location.
- If you are within the coordinates and radius of your assigned office, the **Clock In** or **Clock Out** buttons will unlock.
- Clocking in logs your precise time and location details.

### 3. Accomplishment Logs
- Document your daily deliverables in the log form.
- Attach links or screenshots representing your work outputs.
- Submit logs for your Supervisor's review.

---

## School Coordinator Console

School Coordinators act as the liaison between the school and SDO QC offices:
- **Student Roster**: View the list of students under your supervision.
- **Placement Monitoring**: Check which office/department each student has been deployed to.
- **Attendance Summaries**: Access reports summarizing clock-in times, lates, and total completed hours for your students.
